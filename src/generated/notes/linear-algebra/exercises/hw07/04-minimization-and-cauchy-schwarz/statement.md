(30 points) Let $V$ be a real inner product space and let $v,w\in V$ be nonzero vectors. Consider the function $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ given by

$$
f(t)=|v-tw|^2.
$$

:::list

1.  **4.1.** (10 points) Prove that $f$ has a unique minimum.

2.  **4.2.** (10 points) Find the minimum value of $f$ and the value of $t$ for which $f(t)$ equals the minimum value.

3.  **4.3.** (10 points) Use your solution to the previous parts of this problem to prove that for any $v,w\in V$,

    $$
    (v,w)^2\le(v,v)(w,w)
    $$

    and equality holds if and only if there exists a scalar $s\in\mathbb{R}$ such that

    $$
    v=sw\quad\text{or}\quad w=sv.
    $$

:::
