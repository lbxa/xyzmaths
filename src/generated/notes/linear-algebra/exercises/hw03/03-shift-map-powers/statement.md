Let $E=\begin{bmatrix}e_1&e_2&e_3\end{bmatrix}$ be a basis of $V$. Consider the linear map $L:V\to V$ such that

$$
\begin{aligned}
      L(e_1)&=e_2\\
      L(e_2)&=e_3\\
      L(e_3)&=0
    \end{aligned}
$$

Show that if $M$ is the matrix where $L(E)=EM$, then $M,M^2\ne0$ but $M^3=0$.
