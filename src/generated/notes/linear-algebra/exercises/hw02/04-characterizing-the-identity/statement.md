:::list

1.  **(a)** Fix $s\ge1$. Prove that if $J\in\mathcal M_{r\times r}$ satisfies $JB=B$ for every $B\in\mathcal M_{r\times s}$, then $J=I_{r\times r}$.

2.  **(b)** Show by example that, for every $r\ge2$, $JB=B$ for a single nonzero $B$ does not imply $J=I_{r\times r}$. What happens when $r=1$?

:::
