Let $a,b\ge0$. Prove the arithmetic mean-geometric mean inequality

$$
\sqrt{ab}\le\frac{a+b}{2}
$$

and deduce the necessary and sufficient condition when the inequality above is strict. (Hint: Consider $(\sqrt a-\sqrt b)^2$.)
