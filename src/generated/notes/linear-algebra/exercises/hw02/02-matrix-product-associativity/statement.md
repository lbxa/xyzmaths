Let $A,B,C$ be scalar-valued matrices such that $(AB)C$ and $A(BC)$ are well defined. Use the formula for matrix multiplication in [Exercise 2.1.1](/notes/linear-algebra/linear-algebra-i/matrices-and-vector-coordinates/#ex:hw02:matrix-product-entries) to prove associativity, i.e., $(AB)C=A(BC)$.
