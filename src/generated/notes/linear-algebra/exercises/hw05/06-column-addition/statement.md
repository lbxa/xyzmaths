Let the columns of $M$ be $c_1,\ldots,c_n$. Prove that, given $j\ne k$ and $a\in\mathbb F$, if $M'$ is the matrix whose columns are given by

$$
\begin{aligned}
      c'_j&=c_j+ac_k\\
      c'_i&=c_i\quad\text{if \(i\ne j\)}
    \end{aligned}
$$

then

$$
\det(M')=\det(M).
$$
