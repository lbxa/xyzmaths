Let $f(x)$ be continuous over $\mathbb{R}$ satisfying the additivity condition

$$
f(s+t)=f(s)+f(t),\qquad s,t\in\mathbb{R}.
$$

Show that there is a constant $a$ such that $f(x)=ax$. (Hint: First prove this when $x$ is an integer and identify what $a$ is. Then prove this when $x$ is rational. Finally pass to limit for any real $x$ by using the denseness of $\mathbb{Q}$ in $\mathbb{R}$.)
