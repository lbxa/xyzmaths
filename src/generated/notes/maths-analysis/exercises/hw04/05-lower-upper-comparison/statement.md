Consider

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n,\qquad\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}b_n,
$$

where $a_n\ge0$ and $b_n>0$ for $n=1,2,\ldots$. Define

$$
r=\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{a_n}{b_n},\qquad
    R=\limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{a_n}{b_n}.
$$

:::list

1.  **(i)** Show that if $r>0$ and $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}b_n$ diverges, so does $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$.

2.  **(ii)** Show that if $R<\infty$ and $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}b_n$ converges, so does $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$.

:::
