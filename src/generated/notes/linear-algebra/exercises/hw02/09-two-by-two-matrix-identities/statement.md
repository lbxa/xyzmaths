Let $\mathbb F=\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, and let

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}2&3\\4&-1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
    B=\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&-1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

:::list

1.  **(a)** Compute $AB-BA$.

2.  **(b)** Show that $X\in\mathcal M_{2\times2}$ satisfies $AX=XA$ if and only if $X=pI+qA$ for some $p,q\in\mathbb F$.

3.  **(c)** Show that $X=0$ is the only $X\in\mathcal M_{2\times2}$ with $AX=XB$.

4.  **(d)** Show that $A^2=A+14I$.

5.  **(e)** Use this to write $A^3$ in the form $pA+qI$.

6.  **(f)** Use this to find $N\in\mathcal M_{2\times2}$ such that $AN=NA=I$.

:::
