Let $f(x)$ be a continuous function over an interval $I$, with finite endpoints $a,b$, which may or may not be closed. Prove that if $f(x)$ is uniformly continuous over $I$, then $f$ is bounded. With this result, show that the function

$$
f(x)=\frac{1}{1+x},\qquad x\in(-1,1)
$$

is continuous but not uniformly continuous.
