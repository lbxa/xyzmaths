Decide whether each statement is true or false. Prove every true statement and give an explicit counterexample for every false one. Here $S,T$ are subsets of a vector space $V$ over $\mathbb F$, where $\mathbb F$ denotes $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$.

:::list

1.  **(a)** If $S$ and $T$ are linearly independent, then $S\cup T$ is linearly independent.

2.  **(b)** If $S$ and $T$ are linearly independent, then $S\cap T$ is linearly independent.

3.  **(c)** If $S$ and $T$ are finite and $\operatorname{span}(S)=\operatorname{span}(T)$, then $S$ and $T$ have the same number of elements.

4.  **(d)** $\operatorname{span}(S\cap T)
          =\operatorname{span}(S)\cap\operatorname{span}(T)$.

5.  **(e)** If $S\subsetneq T$ and $T$ is linearly independent, then $\operatorname{span}(S)\subsetneq\operatorname{span}(T)$.

:::
