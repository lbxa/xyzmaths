Let $\{f_n(x)\}$ and $\{g_n(x)\}$ be two uniformly convergent sequences whose limit are $f(x)$ and $g(x)$, respectively.

:::list

1.  **(i)** Show that $f_n(x)g_n(x)\to f(x)g(x)$ as $n\to\infty$ uniformly in $C[a,b]$ (for finite $a,b$).

2.  **(ii)** Give an example to show that (i) may fail in $C(-\infty,\infty)$.

3.  **(iii)** Show that $f_n(x)\pm g_n(x)\to f(x)\pm g(x)$ as $n\to\infty$ uniformly regardless of the underlying interval.

:::
