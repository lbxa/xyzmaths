Consider the sequence

$$
f_n(x)=\frac{nx}{1+n^2x^2},\qquad
    x\in[0,1],\quad n=1,2,\ldots.
$$

:::list

1.  **(a)** Show that $f_n(x)\to0$ pointwise as $n\to\infty$.

2.  **(b)** Show that the convergence is not uniform.

:::
