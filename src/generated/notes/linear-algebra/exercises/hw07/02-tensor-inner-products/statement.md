Let $V$ be a 2-dimensional real vector space and $(e_1,e_2)$ be a basis. Which of the following 2-tensors is an inner product? Explain.

:::list

1.  **2.1.** (5 points) Let $T:V\times V\to\mathbb{R}$ be the 2-tensor given by

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
              T(e_1,e_1)&=1\\
              T(e_1,e_2)=T(e_2,e_1)&=1\\
              T(e_2,e_2)&=1
            \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  **2.2.** (5 points) Let $T:V\times V\to\mathbb{R}$ be given by

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
              T(e_1,e_1)&=1\\
              T(e_1,e_2)&=1\\
              T(e_2,e_1)&=-1\\
              T(e_2,e_2)&=1
            \end{aligned}
    $$

:::
