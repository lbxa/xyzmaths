For $x\in\mathbb{R}_+$, we defined the function $x^{1/n}$ for $n\in\mathbb{N}$.

:::list

1.  **(a)** For $x,y\in\mathbb{R}_+$, prove that $(xy)^{1/n}=x^{1/n}y^{1/n}$.

2.  **(b)** For $x\in\mathbb{R}_+$, we defined $x^{m/n}=(x^m)^{1/n}$ for $m\in\mathbb{N}$. Show that this is the same as $(x^{1/n})^m$.

3.  **(c)** For $x\in\mathbb{R}_+$ and $r,s\in\mathbb{Q}_+$, prove the laws of exponents

    $$
    (x^r)^s=x^{rs},\qquad x^{r+s}=x^rx^s.
    $$

:::
