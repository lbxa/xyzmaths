Use the exponential addition law

$$
E(x+y)=E(x)E(y)\quad\text{or}\quad
    e^{x+y}=e^x e^y,\qquad x,y\in\mathbb{R}.
$$

:::list

1.  **(i)** Show that

    $$
    E(mx)=E(x)^m,\qquad E\left(\frac{x}{m}\right)=E(x)^{1/m},
            \qquad x\in\mathbb{R},\quad m\in\mathbb{N}.
    $$

2.  **(ii)** Show that (i) can be extended to

    $$
    E(qx)=E(x)^q,\qquad q\in\mathbb{Q},\quad x\in\mathbb{R}.
    $$

3.  **(iii)** Show that (ii) can be extended to

    $$
    E(ax)=E(x)^a,\qquad a\in\mathbb{R},\quad x\in\mathbb{R}.
    $$

:::
