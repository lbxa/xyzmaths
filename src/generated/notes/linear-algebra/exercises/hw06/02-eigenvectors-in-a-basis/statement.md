Let $(e_1,e_2,e_3)$ be a basis of $V$ and $L:V\to V$ be the map given by

$$
\begin{aligned}
      L(e_1)&=e_1+e_2\\
      L(e_2)&=2e_2+e_3\\
      L(e_3)&=2e_3.
    \end{aligned}
$$

Find the eigenvectors of $L$ by solving

$$
L(v)=\lambda v,
$$

where

$$
v=e_1a^1+e_2a^2+e_3a^3,
$$

directly for $a^1,a^2,a^3,\lambda$.
