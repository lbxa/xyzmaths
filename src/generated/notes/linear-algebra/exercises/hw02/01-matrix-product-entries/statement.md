Given a matrix $M$, let $M^j_k$ denote the entry in the $j$-th row and $k$-th column of $M$. Let $A\in\mathcal M_{r\times s}$ and $B\in\mathcal M_{s\times t}$. Show that the definition of matrix multiplication is the same as

$$
(AB)^j_l=\sum_{k=1}^s A^j_kB^k_l,
    \qquad 1\le j\le r,\quad 1\le l\le t.
$$
