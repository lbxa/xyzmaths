Let $\mathbb F$ denote $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. In $\mathbb F^3$, define

$$
\begin{aligned}
      H&=\{(x,y,z):x+y+z=0,\ x-y=0\},\\
      A&=\{(x,y,z):x+y+z=1,\ x-y=0\}.
    \end{aligned}
$$

:::list

1.  **(a)** Prove that $H$ is a subspace and find a basis and its dimension. Determine whether $A$ is a subspace.

2.  **(b)** Find $p\in A$ for which $A=\{p+h:h\in H\}$, and prove this equality. Prove that the difference of any two elements of $A$ belongs to $H$.

:::
