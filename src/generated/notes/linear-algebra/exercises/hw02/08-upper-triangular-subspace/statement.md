Prove that the set of upper triangular matrices

$$
\{A\in\mathcal M_{r\times r}:A^j_k=0\text{ whenever }j>k\}
$$

is a subspace of $\mathcal M_{r\times r}$, compute its dimension, and prove that it is closed under matrix multiplication.
