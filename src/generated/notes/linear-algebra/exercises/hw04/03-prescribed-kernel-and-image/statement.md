Define the subspaces

$$
K=\operatorname{span}\{(1,1,0),(0,1,1)\}\subseteq\mathbb{R}^3
$$

and

$$
S=\operatorname{span}\{(1,2,1)\}\subseteq\mathbb{R}^3.
$$

Construct a linear map $L:\mathbb{R}^3\to\mathbb{R}^3$ such that

$$
\ker L=K\qquad\text{and}\qquad\operatorname{im}L=S.
$$

:::list

1.  **(a)** Give an explicit formula for $L(x,y,z)$.

2.  **(b)** Write $L$ as a matrix.

3.  **(c)** Prove that its kernel and image are exactly $K$ and $S$, rather than merely containing these subspaces.

4.  **(d)** Verify rank-nullity for your map.

:::
