Explain why the determinant of an upper triangular $m\times m$ matrix equals the product of its diagonal entries. In other words, explain why

$$
\det\begin{bmatrix}
      A^1_1&A^1_2&\cdots&A^1_{m-1}&A^1_m\\
      0&A^2_2&\cdots&A^2_{m-1}&A^2_m\\
      \vdots&&&&\vdots\\
      0&0&\cdots&0&A^m_m
    \end{bmatrix}=A^1_1 A^2_2\cdots A^m_m.
$$

A detailed proof is not required. Describe the process that gives this conclusion.
