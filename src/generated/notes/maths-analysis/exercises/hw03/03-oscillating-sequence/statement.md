Consider the sequence

$$
a_n=\frac{1}{n}+\sin\left(\frac{2\pi n}{3}\right),
    \qquad n=1,2,\ldots.
$$

Find all limit points of $\{a_n\}$ and obtain

$$
\liminf_{n\to\infty}a_n,\quad\limsup_{n\to\infty}a_n.
$$
