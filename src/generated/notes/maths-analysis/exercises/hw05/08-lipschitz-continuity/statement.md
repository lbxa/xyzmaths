Consider a function $f(x)$ over $\mathbb{R}$. We say that $f(x)$ is Lipschitzian if there is a constant $L>0$ such that

$$
|f(x)-f(y)|\le L|x-y|,\qquad x,y\in\mathbb{R}.
$$

Here $L$ is called the Lipschitz constant of $f$.

:::list

1.  **(a)** Prove that a Lipschitz function is uniformly continuous over the entire $\mathbb{R}$.

2.  **(b)** Consider the function

    $$
    f(x)=\frac{1}{1+x^2},\qquad x\in\mathbb{R}.
    $$

    Show that this function is Lipschitzian and estimate its Lipschitzian constant.

:::
