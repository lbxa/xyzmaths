Consider $A=\{a\in\mathbb{R}\mid a^3>5-\sqrt{2}\}$.

:::list

1.  **(a)** Find $B\subseteq\mathbb{R}$ such that $\{A,B\}$ forms a Dedekind partition.

2.  **(b)** Find the Dedekind cut for this partition.

3.  **(c)** Show that $A$ is bounded below and $B$ is bounded above and find

    $$
    \inf A\quad\text{and}\quad\sup B.
    $$

:::
