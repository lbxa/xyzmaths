The Fibonacci sequence $\{F_n\}$ is defined by

$$
F_0=0,\quad F_1=1,\quad F_n=F_{n-2}+F_{n-1},
    \qquad n=2,3,\ldots.
$$

:::list

1.  **(a)** Show that $\{F_n\}$ is an increasing sequence. *Here increasing means nondecreasing.*

2.  **(b)** Show that $\{F_n\}$ is unbounded.

3.  **(c)** Show that the Fibonacci ratios

    $$
    r_n=\frac{F_n}{F_{n-1}},\qquad n=2,3,\ldots,
    $$

    is a bounded sequence.

4.  **(d)** Assume the limit of $\{r_n\}$ exists. Find the limit (which happens to be the golden ratio).

:::
