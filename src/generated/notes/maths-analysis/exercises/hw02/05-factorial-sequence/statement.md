Consider the sequence

$$
x_n=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{1}{k!},\quad n=1,2,\ldots,
$$

in $\mathbb{Q}$ and define $S=\{x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n,\ldots\}$ which is a subset of $\mathbb{Q}$.

:::list

1.  **(a)** Show that $S$ is a bounded set.

2.  **(b)** Find $a=\inf S$ and $b=\sup S$.

3.  **(c)** Is it true that $a$ and $b$ both lie in $\mathbb{Q}$? Explain why and why not.

:::
