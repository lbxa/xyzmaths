Show that the function

$$
\left(1+\frac1x\right)^{x+1}
$$

decreases and find the limit

$$
\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(1+\frac1x\right)^{x+1}.
$$

[^43]

:::notes{id="footnotes-42"}

[^43]: The source does not specify a domain. Take $x>0$ for this positive-base real-power problem.

:::
