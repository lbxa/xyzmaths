Consider

$$
E(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^k}{k!},\qquad
    E_n(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{x^k}{k!},\qquad x\in\mathbb{R}.
$$

:::list

1.  **(i)** Establish the absolute and uniform convergence $E_n(x)\to E(x)$ as $n\to\infty$ over any interval $[a,b]$ $(-\infty<a<b<\infty)$.

2.  **(ii)** Use (i) to show that $E(x)$ is continuous.

3.  **(iii)** Show that $\ln x$ $(x>0)$ is continuous.

:::
