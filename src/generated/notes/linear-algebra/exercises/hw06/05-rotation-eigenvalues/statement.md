For any $\alpha\in\mathbb{R}$, the real matrix

$$
R_\alpha=\begin{bmatrix}
      \cos\alpha&-\sin\alpha\\
      \sin\alpha&\cos\alpha
    \end{bmatrix}
$$

is called a rotation matrix, because it rotates each vector counterclockwise by the angle $\alpha$. Compute the (possibly complex) eigenvalues and eigenvectors of $R_\alpha$.
