On $V=\{x\in\mathbb{R}:x>0\}$, define

$$
x\oplus y=xy,\qquad a\odot x=x^a\qquad(a\in\mathbb{R}).
$$

:::list

1.  **(a)** Prove that these operations make $V$ a vector space over $\mathbb{R}$. Identify its zero vector and the additive inverse of $x$.

2.  **(b)** Determine the dimension of $V$ and characterize all its bases.

:::

You may use the usual properties of real powers and logarithms.
