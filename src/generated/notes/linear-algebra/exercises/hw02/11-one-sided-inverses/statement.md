Let $A,B\in\mathcal M_{m\times m}$ with $AB=I$.

:::list

1.  **(a)** Prove that if $x$ is a column matrix such that $Bx=0$, then $x=0$.

2.  **(b)** Deduce that the columns of $B$ are distinct and form a linearly independent subset of $\mathbb F^m$ with $m$ elements, and hence a basis. Conclude that every $y\in\mathbb F^m$ can be written as $y=Bx$.

3.  **(c)** Prove that $BA=I$.

:::
