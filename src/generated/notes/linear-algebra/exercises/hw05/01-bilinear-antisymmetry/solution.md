:::div{class="proof"}

*Solution.* Let $v,w\in V$ be arbitrary. By the hypothesis and bilinearity,

$$
\begin{aligned}
  0&=A(v+w,v+w)\\
   &=A(v,v)+A(v,w)+A(w,v)+A(w,w).
\end{aligned}
$$

Since $A(v,v)=A(w,w)=0$, this reduces to

$$
A(v,w)+A(w,v)=0.
$$

Hence $A(w,v)=-A(v,w)$. Since $v,w$ were arbitrary, $A$ is antisymmetric. ◻
