Use the existence of the Dedekind cut in $\mathbb{R}$ to prove the greatest lower bound property. That is, if $S$ is a nonempty subset of $\mathbb{R}$ which is bounded below, then there is a unique number $c\in\mathbb{R}$ such that

$$
c=\inf S.
$$

(Do not use LUBP.)
