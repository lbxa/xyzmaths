# Appendix A. Notation {id="ch:appendix:notation"}

This reference collects notation used in the book. Definitions and hypotheses are given in the corresponding mathematical sections.

## Sets and number systems {id="sets-and-number-systems"}

$\mathbb{N},\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Q},\mathbb{R},\mathbb{C}$  
Natural numbers, integers, rational numbers, real numbers, and complex numbers. Here $\mathbb{N}=\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$.

$A\subseteq B$, $A\subsetneq B$  
Set inclusion and strict inclusion.

$\mathcal P(A)$, $|A|$  
The power set and cardinality of $A$.

$A^c$  
Complement relative to a specified universe.

$f:A\to B$, $f(A)$  
A function with domain $A$ and codomain $B$, and its image. For a bijection, $f^{-1}$ denotes its inverse.

$\aleph_0$, $\mathfrak c$  
Countable infinity and the cardinality of $\mathbb{R}$. The symbol $\aleph$ in some exercises also denotes $\mathfrak c$.

$\mathcal A$, $\mathcal T$  
Real algebraic numbers and real transcendental numbers in the number-system overview.

## Order and approximation {id="order-and-approximation"}

$|x|$  
Absolute value.

$\sup S$, $\inf S$  
Least upper bound and greatest lower bound.

$\lfloor x\rfloor$  
The greatest integer not exceeding $x$.

$\overline E$  
Closure of a set $E$.

## Sequences and series {id="sequences-and-series"}

$x_n\to L$, $x_{n_k}$  
Convergence to $L$ and a subsequence with strictly increasing indices $n_k$.

$\liminf x_n$, $\limsup x_n$  
Limits of tail infima and suprema. Infinite values are allowed where explicitly stated.

$S_N=\sum_{n=0}^N a_n$  
A partial sum. The initial index may instead be $1$, as specified in each series.

## Functions and calculus {id="functions-and-calculus"}

$f'(c)$, $f'_-(c)$, $f'_+(c)$  
Derivative, left derivative, and right derivative at $c$.

$f^{(n)}$, $C^n[a,b]$  
The $n$th derivative and functions whose derivatives through order $n$ extend continuously to $[a,b]$.

$r(h)=o(h)$  
The condition $r(h)/h\to0$ as $h\to0$ through nonzero values.

$C[a,b]$, $\|f\|_\infty$  
Continuous real-valued functions on $[a,b]$ and their supremum norm $\max_{x\in[a,b]}|f(x)|$.

$e^x$, $\ln x$  
The exponential function and its inverse on positive inputs.
