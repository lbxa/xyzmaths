Let $T:\mathbb{R}^4\to\mathbb{R}^3$ be the linear map given by

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}
      1&2&0&1\\
      0&1&1&1\\
      1&3&1&2
    \end{bmatrix}.
$$

:::list

1.  **(a)** Find $\ker T$ and give a basis for it.

2.  **(b)** Find $\operatorname{im}T$ and give a basis for it.

3.  **(c)** Compute the nullity and rank of $T$, and verify the rank-nullity theorem.

4.  **(d)** Determine whether $T$ is injective and whether it is surjective. Justify both answers.

:::
