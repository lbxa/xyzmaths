Which of the following is an inner product? Explain.

:::list

1.  **1.1.** (5 points) For any $(v^1,v^2),(w^1,w^2)\in\mathbb{R}^2$,

    $$
    (v,w)=v^1w^2+v^2w^1.
    $$

2.  **1.2.** (5 points) For any $(v^1,v^2),(w^1,w^2)\in\mathbb{R}^2$,

    $$
    (v,w)=2v^1w^1-v^1w^2-v^2w^1+2v^2w^2.
    $$

:::
