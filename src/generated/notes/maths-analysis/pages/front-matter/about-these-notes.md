# About these notes {id="about-these-notes" class="unnumbered"}

This book develops the foundations of mathematical analysis. The foundations chapter progresses from sets and cardinality to completeness, density, and rational powers. The chapters on sequences and number series develop convergence, with power-series examples in the chapter on sequences and series of functions. Results stated without proof are explicitly identified.

The main text contains the definitions and complete arguments. Margin notes provide reminders, secondary observations, and small diagrams. Examples quoted from later mathematics are identified when their proofs are outside the present scope.
