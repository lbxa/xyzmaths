Give an example of a sequence of nested nonempty bounded intervals $\{I_n\}$ in $\mathbb{R}$ such that $I_{n+1}\subset I_n$ for each $n=1,2,\ldots$ but

$$
\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty} I_n=\varnothing.
$$

Explain what goes “wrong”.
