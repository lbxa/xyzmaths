Let $\mathbb F$ denote $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. Let $V$ be a vector space over $\mathbb F$, $v\in V$, and $a\in\mathbb F$. Prove the following statements directly from the vector space axioms.

:::list

1.  **(a)** The zero vector and the additive inverse of each vector are unique.

2.  **(b)** $0v=0_V$ and $a0_V=0_V$.

3.  **(c)** $(-1)v=-v$.

4.  **(d)** If $av=0_V$, then $a=0$ or $v=0_V$.

:::

Distinguish scalar zeros from vector zeros in your proofs.
