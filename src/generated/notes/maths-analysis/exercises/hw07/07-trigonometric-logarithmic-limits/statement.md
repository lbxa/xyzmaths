Find the limits

$$
\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin ax}{x},\qquad
    \lim_{x\to0}\frac{x}{\ln(1+ax)},\qquad
    \lim_{x\to0}\frac{1-\cos2x}{\sin^2x}.
$$

The second quotient requires $a\ne0$. If $a=0$, its denominator is identically zero.
