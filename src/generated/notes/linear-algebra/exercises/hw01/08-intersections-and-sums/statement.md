Let $\mathbb F$ denote $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. In $\mathbb F^4$, let

$$
\begin{aligned}
      U&=\operatorname{span}\{(1,0,1,0),(0,1,0,1)\},\\
      W&=\operatorname{span}\{(1,1,0,0),(0,0,1,1)\}.
    \end{aligned}
$$

:::list

1.  **(a)** Find bases and dimensions of $U$, $W$, and $U\cap W$.

2.  **(b)** Define $U+W=\{u+w:u\in U,w\in W\}$. Prove directly that $U+W$ is a subspace, find a basis and its dimension, and describe it by one linear equation. Do not assume a dimension formula for sums of subspaces.

:::
