Consider the sequence

$$
f_n(x)=nx(1-x)^n,\qquad n\in\mathbb{N},\quad x\in[0,1].
$$

:::list

1.  **(i)** Show that $f_n(x)\to0$ as $n\to\infty$ pointwise. (Hint: Use [Exercise 8.6.9](/notes/maths-analysis/analysis-i/sequences-and-series-of-functions/#ex:hw06:arbitrary-base-exponential) and [Exercise 8.6.12](/notes/maths-analysis/analysis-i/sequences-and-series-of-functions/#ex:hw06:exponential-dominates-powers).)

2.  **(ii)** Show that the convergence $f_n(x)\to0$ as $n\to\infty$ is not uniform. (Hint: Try $x_n=\frac1{n+1}$ or use Calculus to get where $f_n(x)$ attains its maximum.)

:::
