Let $L:\mathbb{R}^3\to\mathbb{R}^2$ be defined by

$$
L(x,y,z)=(x+y+z,\;x+2y+3z).
$$

:::list

1.  **(a)** Find a basis for $\ker L$.

2.  **(b)** Extend this basis to an ordered basis $E=(e_1,e_2,e_3)$ of $\mathbb{R}^3$, with $e_1$ spanning $\ker L$.

3.  **(c)** Choose an ordered basis $F=(f_1,f_2)$ of $\mathbb{R}^2$ such that

    $$
    L(e_2)=f_1,\qquad L(e_3)=f_2.
    $$

4.  **(d)** Find the matrix $M$ of $L$ with respect to $E$ and $F$.

5.  **(e)** Verify directly the identity

    $$
    L(E)=FM.
    $$

:::
