There are nonzero matrices $A$ and $B$ such that $AB=0$.

:::list

1.  **(a)** Find all $A\in\mathcal M_{2\times2}$ with $A^2=0$.

2.  **(b)** Find $A,B\in\mathcal M_{2\times2}$ with $AB=0$ and $BA\ne0$.

:::
