(10 points) Let $V_1,V_2$ be subspaces of $V$ such that

$$
V_1\cap V_2=\{0\}.
$$

Prove directly that whenever $v_1\in V_1$ and $v_2\in V_2$ are nonzero vectors, the set $\{v_1,v_2\}$ is linearly independent.
