Let $V$ be a vector space over $\mathbb F$, where $\mathbb F$ denotes $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. Let $S,T\subseteq V$ be arbitrary subsets, possibly empty or infinite. Recall that an empty linear combination is equal to $0_V$.

:::list

1.  **(a)** Prove that $\operatorname{span}(S)$ is a subspace containing $S$.

2.  **(b)** Prove that every subspace containing $S$ contains $\operatorname{span}(S)$. Deduce that $\operatorname{span}(S)$ is the intersection of all subspaces containing $S$.

:::
