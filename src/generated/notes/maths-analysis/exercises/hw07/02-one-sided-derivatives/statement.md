Consider the function

$$
f(x)=\begin{cases}
      \displaystyle\frac{x}{1+e^{1/x}},&x\ne0,\\[4pt]
      0,&x=0.
    \end{cases}
$$

:::list

1.  **(i)** Show that $f$ is continuous at $x=0$.

2.  **(ii)** Find the left- and right-derivatives of $f$ at $x=0$.

:::
