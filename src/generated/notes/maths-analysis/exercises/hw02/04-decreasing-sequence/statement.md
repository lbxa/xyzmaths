Consider the seqeunce

$$
x_n=\frac{1}{n^2}+\frac{2}{n^2}+\cdots+\frac{n}{n^2},
    \quad n=1,2,\ldots.
$$

:::list

1.  **(a)** Show that it is monotone decreasing and bounded below.

2.  **(b)** Show that it is Cauchy.

3.  **(c)** Obtain the limit of $\{x_n\}$.

4.  **(d)** Let $S=\{x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n,\ldots\}$. Obtain both $\inf S$ and $\sup S$.

:::
