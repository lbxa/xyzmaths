For $x\in\mathbb{R}$, investigate the absolute convergence of the power series

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{x^n}{(\sqrt3)^n n^k},
$$

where $k$ is an integer using the ratio test or root test for convergence or divergence.
