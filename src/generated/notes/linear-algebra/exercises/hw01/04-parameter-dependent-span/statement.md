Let $\mathbb F$ denote $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. For $a\in\mathbb F$, set

$$
v_1=(1,0,1),\qquad v_2=(0,1,1),\qquad v_3=(1,1,a).
$$

:::list

1.  **(a)** Determine exactly when $\{v_1,v_2,v_3\}$ is a basis of $\mathbb F^3$. For each remaining value of $a$, find a basis and the dimension of its span, and describe the span by a linear equation.

2.  **(b)** When the three vectors form a basis, express any $(x,y,z)\in\mathbb F^3$ as their linear combination.

:::
